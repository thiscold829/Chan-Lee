How

For each screen I first decide how it should behave and look, then keep revising it with AI coding tools until it does. Documents and films are made the same way. I set the standard and check the result.

Work system

Every task runs under the same rules, and decisions, formats and past mistakes are kept in memory files so that they carry into the next task. On that base I use, every day, a work-report tool, builders for decks, reports and screen specifications, minutes drafted from recordings, and automated HWP editing.

App showcase

Cardin

Business-card app I use in place of Remember: card recognition built into the app, with no AI calls

What stands out

  • Take a photo of a card and it finds the corners, straightens it and splits name, company and contacts into fields, automatically
  • Finding, cropping and field-splitting built into the app, with text read by a Google feature, so no AI tokens or call costs however many cards you scan
  • The same card book on phone and desktop, ad-free, with add, edit and delete

Main functions

  • On-phone card detection with no outside library: edges, lines, best quadrilateral, perspective correction
  • Second pass for shadows and backgrounds close to the card’s colour
  • In-app camera: corners marked automatically, landscape or portrait set on its own
  • Text read by Google Drive OCR
  • Call, message, e-mail or save as contact; duplicate warning
  • Batch upload from the album, resumed after a dropped connection

WeFIN

Rules

The rules applied to every task.

  1. State only what has been verified. Every sentence needs a source.
  2. Report before working. Say what will change, then change it.
  3. Revise the latest version. Do not start over without a reason.
  4. Check every deliverable for errors before it goes out.
  5. Take the best app in the same field as the benchmark, from its real screens.
  6. Make it usable by a first-time user without explanation.
