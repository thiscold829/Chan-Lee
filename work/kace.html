KACE: AI nutrient estimation, a selected national R&D project

Proposal, evaluation deck and four-year roadmap for estimating the nutrients of processed foods with AI, and the first year’s research.

Client
Ministry of Food and Drug Safety. Consortium: Cheminet, KAIST, Seoul National University, Sejong University, KOTITI
Period
Proposal 2025. Selected; project February 2026 to November 2029, year one of four
Role
Proposal and evaluation deck with the team, roadmap design, year-one research
Deliverables
Proposal, evaluation deck, four-year roadmap, kickoff deck; year-one research and a dashboard prototype

The proposal

Five institutions took part in the proposal. I merged each partner’s manuscript into a single deck with one voice and one visual system, and drew the roadmap that shows what every year delivers. After selection I prepared the kickoff deck.

Year one

KACE estimates the nutrient content of processed foods with big data and AI. In the first year I am doing the groundwork for trustworthy estimates.

  • Designed the prompts and ran the first prediction tests, starting with calcium and saturated fat in representative foods
  • Mapped ingredient synonyms and built the keyword set that cleans manufacturer names
  • Reviewed prior studies on AI nutrient prediction and the registration procedure for literature reviews
  • Built a dashboard prototype on the project’s full product dataset, with missing values shown as they are
