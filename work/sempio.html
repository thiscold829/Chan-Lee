Culinary research at Sempio

Ingredient research for the cooking science team, and a Sebasi talk on cooking science planned and scripted end to end, published on YouTube.

Where
Sempio Foods, culinary research team
Period
February to August 2018
Role
Researcher, culinary research mentee program (fifth class)
Deliverables
Ingredient studies and a research record; a Sebasi talk; press conference and lecture decks; a research booklet
The Sebasi talk on YouTube

Research

Sempio’s culinary research team studies Korean food culture and cooking science. I researched traditional food culture, studied ingredient characteristics in the cooking science team and reviewed them for product use, and kept the experiment records, such as how seaweed changes as it heats.

Sebasi talk

For the Sebasi × Sempio collaboration lecture I planned a fifteen-minute talk on cooking science from a blank page: the argument, the script and every slide. Following a recipe is not the same as knowing how to cook; the talk shows why a recipe fails when the ingredients change, what cooking science explains about those changes, and proves it with one spring herb. Sempio’s head chef delivered it on 6 July 2018, and it was published on Sebasi’s YouTube channel.
