TripTote

A travel app that keeps the itinerary, route, shared spending and packing list in one place. I planned and designed it, built it with AI coding tools and run it, server included.

Planners plan, wanderers pickShared moneyTravelling togetherDesign and operation
Where
Personal project
Role
Concept, screen and design decisions, operation. Built with AI coding tools
Period
November 2026 to present
Form
Web app for desktop and phone, installable on the home screen, works offline
Address
triptote.pages.dev

Planners plan, wanderers pick

Planners put places into each day and fit the route on the map, reordering by drag or by distance, with a warning when a place will be closed. Wanderers collect places in a bag and pull them out as they go, or let the app pick one nearby that is open now.

Open TripTote

Shared money

Spending is recorded in the local currency and in won, split by who paid and whose share it is, and settled. An existing ledger imports as a CSV file; the itinerary saves as an image or PDF.

Travelling together

It works straight away without an account. With a Kakao or Google sign-in, a trip continues on another device and companions join through an invitation link. Edits made at the same time are merged item by item, and the app keeps working offline.

Design and operation

Real photographs of each place, a map coloured by hand and a single accent colour, petrol. The server and database run on Cloudflare’s free tier, and sign-in takes only a nickname and profile picture.
