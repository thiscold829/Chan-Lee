K-HBM: from survey screens to a live platform

Designed the survey tool now used in three government research projects, led acceptance testing and specified the administrator screens of a national platform in operation. Two conference posters, both awarded.

VeST, the survey tool

VeST replaces paper dietary surveys with a web and mobile tool: a 24-hour dietary record and an interactive food frequency questionnaire. I turned the researchers’ requirements into survey structure, question flow and input methods, designed the participant and administrator screens, and iterated them on feedback from the faculty and field staff. Three government research projects have used it.

Testing and operations

I tested every release before it reached participants and handled problems while surveys were live. In the acceptance round I led, 30 issues were found and logged before launch. I also wrote and edited my sections of the final report and the evaluation deck.

Administrator screens

For the analysis screens of the DX Insight Center I designed how administrators manage the data behind them: one administrator button on each screen that opens a dialog, with numbered descriptions and input rules for every element. The specification is under review.

The demo film

An 89-second film made for the final evaluation, showing the platform as a user sees it.
