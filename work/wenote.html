WE:NOTE

A web app that compares wedding costs, from the hall to photography, on the couple’s own terms, built on real quotes. I gathered and cleaned the quote data, planned and designed it, and built it with AI coding tools.

Structure

Halls for the wedding venue, rings, studio-dress-makeup, suits, photography, invitations and a checklist, entered from a single lobby.

Open WE:NOTE

Like-for-like prices

The prices are built from real wedding quotes, gathered and cleaned, with personal details removed. Given the region, month, day, time and guaranteed guests, each hall is marked cheap, typical or expensive against quotes on the same terms, and a quote already received can be rated the same way.

Choosing and recording

On the shortlist, the bride and groom score each hall separately and record consultations and on-the-day contract terms to compare side by side. The tour checklist comes as PDF or Excel, and rings are priced from live gold prices.

Invitations

Wedding invitations and invitations to the families’ first meeting each have their own print layout, an A5 card folded into four pages, saved as PDF or printed directly.

Design and operation

Screens carry names and numbers, with one accent colour, mauve rose. User feedback added a budget filter, studio-dress-makeup totals and the tour checklist. A Kakao or Google sign-in carries everything across two devices and lets couples submit their own quotes.
