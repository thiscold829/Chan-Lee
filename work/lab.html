2024: full screen redesign

In the 2024 upgrade I redesigned every user and administrator screen. Integrated search and resource records, resource registration and management, distribution and deposit requests, digital image resources and saved resources went into the specification, with the fields and input rules of each screen.

2025: menus and database

After years of additions the system carried menus that were no longer used and tables that held no data. As the project’s IT service planner I wrote the execution plan and restructured the whole menu: of 38 items, the 10 most used were kept, 17 similar ones were merged and 11 unused ones were removed, leaving five sections with 20 items. Once the empty and unused tables were cleared, 200 database tables became 85.

Maintenance

Since 2025 I have run the maintenance contract: the execution plan, regular releases, monthly security checks, and the monthly and final reports. I also designed new screens such as the untreated rodent resources page and code management.
