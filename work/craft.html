Design, film and photography

A demonstration film for the K-HBM platform, two documentaries, print and photography: production handled in-house, from planning to final cut.

Period

Deliverables

Recognition
Top prize, video category, World Friends Korea youth volunteer contest, 2018

MENTBOX

A fifteen-minute documentary on the fifth class of Sempio’s culinary research mentees, made for their completion ceremony. It follows the class from their first day at Sempio to a kelp study on the farms of Wando and the final farewell, told as a story rather than a highlight reel. I planned, directed, shot and edited it; fellow mentees helped with camera and script. The clip is the opening, in its original cut.

Kir-wa Sseom-tada

Seven minutes on five months with a volunteer team in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan: a school with no classes for us at first, and how that changed. Planned, shot and edited by me; top prize in the video category of the World Friends Korea youth volunteer contest.

K-HBM demonstration

An 89-second demonstration of K-HBM, the national human-biomonitoring data platform, made for its final evaluation: full screen throughout, no zooms or speed-ups, each step held long enough to read. Earlier system and demonstration films for other public systems are in the strip below.

Print and photography

A menu card for a Sempio × Google event in April 2018: designed, hand-assembled with paper, twine and lavender, and photographed. Still lifes for lectures, photographs and video on the set of a Yondu commercial, and the monthly newsletter, posters and banners of the Seo-gu support center.
