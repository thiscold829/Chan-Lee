Mushroom Week, Sempio

A project Sempio’s culinary research team gave me on a new contract after my mentee term ended, on the strength of my earlier work. For its Mushroom Week in October 2018, chefs from six restaurants, among them Choi Hyun-seok (Choi Dot), Kang Mingoo (Mingles) and Cho Hee-sook (Hansikgonggan), cooked from the team’s mushroom research, and I made the event film and a film for each of the six chefs. I handled the planning, the chef interviews, the editing and the music, shaping the interviews, music and cut to each chef’s character, cooking and style.

MENTBOX

A fifteen-minute documentary made for the completion ceremony of Sempio’s fifth class of culinary research mentees. I planned, scripted, shot and edited it on my own; fellow mentees took part on camera. It follows the class from the first day at Sempio to a kelp study on the farms of Wando and the final farewell.

Kir-wa Sseom-tada

Half a year with a volunteer team in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan: a school with no classes for us at first, and how that changed. Planned, shot and edited by me; top prize in the video category of the World Friends Korea youth volunteer contest.

K-HBM demonstration

An 89-second demonstration of K-HBM, the national human-biomonitoring data platform, made for its final evaluation: full screen throughout, no zooms or speed-ups, each step held long enough to read. Earlier system and demonstration films for other public systems are in the strip below.

Print and photography

A menu card for a Sempio × Google event in April 2018: designed, hand-assembled with paper, twine and lavender, and photographed. Still lifes for lectures, photographs and video on the set of a Yondu commercial, and the monthly newsletter, posters and banners of the Seo-gu support center.
