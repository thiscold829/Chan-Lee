The museum

An online museum where anyone, anywhere, can see and hear the literature, art and music of all humanity, free of charge. ‘Decentralized’ means that the works belong to no nation or institution but to everyone. The emblem is a red seal of the character 中, ‘centre’, with its central stroke taken out.

Literature

Jikji Hall

Jikji Hall is an e-book library of world classics, twentieth-century classics, children’s stories and Korean literature, each book in a cover I designed to suit its story. Every book is in English, and works first written in Russian, French, German or Italian are also in the original. Foreign works can be read in the museum’s Korean translations, Korean works in its English translations.

Each translation starts from a design document fixing forms of address, register and terminology, and is checked against the original paragraph by paragraph. Every departure from the original goes into a review table before the book is published.

Art

Bangudae Hall

Bangudae Hall is the art gallery, with four special exhibitions: The Whale-Road, Eyes on the Universe, Masterpieces for Everyone and Wherever They Are. Each has its own theme, choice of works and sequence of rooms, and a docent’s commentary for every work. The posters are designed by the director.

Music

Jongmyo Hall

Jongmyo Hall is the music hall. It holds Korean music, including the Jongmyo ritual music, and Western classical music as albums.

How it runs

The museum is run by one director and a team of AI staff: curators, translators, a keeper for Mumunam and an outreach lead, all working under the same rules. I decide the names, principles, screens and wording, and review the results. Anything that states a fact is published only after it has been checked against a source table.

The docent is named the AI docent Nuri so that the voice is declared as machine-made. When the outside archive for the sound files closed, the files moved to Sound Archives, opened on GitHub. Readers’ suggestions brought in twentieth-century classics.

Principles

Only works free of copyright restrictions are held. Each book’s colophon gives the life dates of its author, editor and illustrator and its source texts, and photographs are credited on their terms of use. Everything can be seen without an account, and no email address or password is asked for.
